My Story

My career began in finance, but over time it evolved into something much bigger — understanding how businesses truly operate beneath the numbers.

Starting in healthcare and progressing from Accountant to Chief Accountant, I built a strong foundation in financial management, reporting, and operational discipline. A move to Dubai expanded my exposure to Internal Audit, Management Accounting, business operations, profitability analysis, and performance management while completing ACCA.

Today, as Chief Financial Officer, I lead financial strategy, business performance, governance, reporting transformation, and operational improvement initiatives.

Along the way, one realization became clear: businesses rarely struggle because they lack data. They struggle because information is fragmented and difficult to translate into meaningful decisions.

That realization sparked my journey into Business Intelligence, analytics, and executive reporting — combining finance, operations, and modern analytics to help organizations make better decisions.

My Journey

Finance Leadership, Strategy & Business Intelligence

Over the past 15+ years, I have progressed from financial reporting and accounting roles into executive finance leadership, helping organizations improve profitability, governance, operational performance and strategic decision-making.

My career has evolved beyond traditional finance into finance transformation, business intelligence, internal audit and performance management—enabling leadership teams to gain greater visibility and make more informed business decisions.

Today, as Chief Financial Officer, I lead five functions under one remit — Finance, Compliance & Risk, Procurement, HR and Administration. I partnered with the CEO to take the business from losses to 15% net profitability, and I stay hands-on: I build the models, dashboards and policies myself, so leadership gets answers in days, not weeks.

"Finance is not about reporting the past. It is about enabling better decisions, accelerating growth, and creating lasting value."
